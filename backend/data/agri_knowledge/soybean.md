# Soybean Farming Guide
## Growing Conditions
- **Temperature:** 20-30°C.
- **Rainfall:** 60-100 cm.
- **Soil:** Well-drained, fertile loam.
## Irrigation Guidance
- Crucial during pod filling and flowering.
- Extremely susceptible to waterlogging. Provide good drainage.
## Fertilizer Guidance
- NPK ratio 20:60:40 kg/ha. Legume crop, fixes its own nitrogen after root nodules form.
- Apply Sulphur for better oil content.
## Risk Factors & Diseases
- Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV): Transmitted by whitefly.
- Stem Fly and Girdle Beetle.
