# Wheat Farming Guide
## Growing Conditions
- **Temperature:** 10-15°C at sowing, 21-26°C at ripening. Above 35°C during grain filling causes heat stress and yield loss.
- **Rainfall:** 50-100 cm optimally.
- **Soil:** Well-drained clay loam or loamy soil.
## Irrigation Guidance
- Requires 4-6 irrigations.
- Critical stages: Crown root initiation (21 days after sowing), tillering, booting, flowering, milk, and dough stages.
- If water is scarce, prioritize crown root initiation and flowering stages.
## Fertilizer Guidance
- NPK ratio generally 120:60:40 kg/ha.
- Apply half N and full P & K at sowing. Apply remaining N at first irrigation.
## Risk Factors & Diseases
- Rust (Brown, Yellow, Black): Manage with resistant varieties and fungicides like Propiconazole.
- Karnal Bunt: Fungal disease affecting grain quality.
- Aphids: Sucking pests. Spray Imidacloprid if severe.
