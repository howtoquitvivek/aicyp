# Irrigation Management
## Methods
- **Flood Irrigation:** Traditional, high water waste. Use only when water is abundant.
- **Sprinkler Irrigation:** Ideal for uneven terrain and sandy soils. Not suitable for high wind areas.
- **Drip Irrigation:** Most efficient. Delivers water directly to roots. Highly recommended for cash crops, orchards, and row crops.
## Water Stress Indicators
- Wilting of leaves during early morning.
- Slower growth rate.
- Leaf rolling (especially in cereals like maize and rice).
