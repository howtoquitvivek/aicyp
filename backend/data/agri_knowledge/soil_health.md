# Soil Health Management
## pH Management
- **Acidic Soils (pH < 6.0):** Apply agricultural lime to raise pH.
- **Alkaline Soils (pH > 8.0):** Apply gypsum and add organic matter.
## Soil Organic Carbon (SOC)
- Crucial for retaining moisture and nutrients.
- Minimum recommended SOC is 0.5%. Add FYM, crop residues, or green manure crops (e.g., Dhaincha) to improve it.
## Physical Health
- Avoid excessive tillage which destroys soil structure.
- Deep plough once every 3 years to break hardpans.
