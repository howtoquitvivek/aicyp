# Rice Farming Guide
## Growing Conditions
- **Temperature:** 21-37°C. High temperature and high humidity are favorable.
- **Rainfall:** Requires heavy rainfall (150-300 cm) or assured irrigation.
- **Soil:** Heavy clay soils that can retain water.
## Irrigation Guidance
- Requires standing water (2-5 cm) for most of the vegetative stage.
- Drain water before harvesting to ensure even ripening.
## Fertilizer Guidance
- NPK ratio generally 100:50:50 kg/ha. Split nitrogen application.
- Apply Zinc Sulphate if zinc deficiency is noticed (common in waterlogged soils).
## Risk Factors & Diseases
- Blast: Fungal disease causing diamond-shaped spots. Use Tricyclazole.
- Brown Plant Hopper (BPH): Causes hopper burn.
- Stem Borer: Use granular insecticides.
