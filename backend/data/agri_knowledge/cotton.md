# Cotton Farming Guide
## Growing Conditions
- **Temperature:** 21-30°C. Needs 210 frost-free days.
- **Rainfall:** 50-100 cm.
- **Soil:** Deep black soil (Regur) or alluvial soils.
## Irrigation Guidance
- Crucial at flowering and boll formation stages.
- Avoid waterlogging at all costs; cotton is highly sensitive to excess water.
## Fertilizer Guidance
- High nutrient requirement. NPK ratio 120:60:60 kg/ha.
- Split nitrogen in 3 doses.
## Risk Factors & Diseases
- Pink Bollworm: Major pest. Use Bt cotton varieties or pheromone traps.
- Whitefly: Sucking pest transmitting leaf curl virus.
- Verticillium Wilt: Fungal soil-borne disease.
