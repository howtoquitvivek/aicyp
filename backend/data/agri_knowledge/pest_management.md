# Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
## Principles
- **Cultural Control:** Crop rotation, deep summer ploughing, maintaining field hygiene.
- **Mechanical Control:** Light traps, pheromone traps, yellow sticky cards.
- **Biological Control:** Encourage natural enemies like ladybird beetles, spiders, and parasitoids (e.g., Trichogramma).
- **Chemical Control:** Use only as a last resort when Economic Threshold Levels (ETL) are crossed.
## Common Alerts
- High humidity (>80%) and moderate temperatures strongly favor fungal diseases.
- Dry spells followed by sudden rain can trigger aphid and whitefly outbreaks.
