# Market & Pricing Intelligence
## Selling Strategies
- **Harvest Glut:** Prices typically drop immediately after harvest due to oversupply. If storage is available, wait 2-3 months.
- **Modal Price:** The price at which most transactions happen. Use this as your baseline benchmark, not the maximum price.
- **Minimum Support Price (MSP):** Government guaranteed price. If market price is below MSP, sell to government procurement centers.
## Storage
- Ensure grain moisture is below 10-12% before storing to prevent fungal growth and pest attacks.
- Use hermetic bags or fumigation for long-term storage.
