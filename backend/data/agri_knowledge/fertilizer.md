# Fertilizer Management
## General Guidance
- Always test soil before bulk application.
- Nitrogen (N) promotes leaf and stem growth. Split doses to prevent leaching.
- Phosphorus (P) promotes root development and early growth. Apply entirely as basal dose.
- Potassium (K) promotes disease resistance and grain quality.
## Organic Alternatives
- Use Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or vermicompost to improve soil organic carbon.
- Neem-coated urea slows nitrogen release and increases efficiency.
- Biofertilizers like Rhizobium (for legumes) and Azotobacter (for non-legumes).
